## About Me

I'm an AI researcher at **KAIST COGI Lab**, interested in how biological learning mechanisms can inform more adaptive and robust artificial neural networks.

My research focuses on **continual learning**, especially reducing catastrophic forgetting through ideas inspired by **synaptic plasticity and metaplasticity**.

## Research Interests

- **Continual Learning:** learning sequential tasks while retaining previously acquired knowledge
- **Brain-inspired AI:** translating biological learning principles into computational models
- **Synaptic Metaplasticity:** exploring parameter stability and plasticity in neural networks
- **Representation Learning:** understanding how learned representations evolve across tasks

## Current Research

🔬 **Metaplasticity for Continual Learning**  
Exploring selective weight stabilization and activity-dependent importance measures to balance learning new tasks with preserving existing knowledge.

🧪 **Continual Learning Benchmarks**  
Experimenting with class-incremental and domain-incremental learning on vision datasets.
